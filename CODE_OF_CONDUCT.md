# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free, welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive; assume good faith.
- Give and accept feedback graciously; critique the work, not the person.
- Keep discussions on topic and use the issue forms so problems can be reproduced.
- Respect that people contribute in their free time and may not answer immediately.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal attacks, trolling.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct,
and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation of anyone who violates it.

To report a problem, use GitHub's *Report content* feature or contact the maintainer privately via a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/kraftkater/VoiceNote/security/advisories/new). Reports are handled confidentially.
